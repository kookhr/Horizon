## [Apple Unveils MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display and Touchscreen](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/proled-xdr-display/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Apple has unveiled a new MacBook Pro featuring a PROLED XDR OLED display and, for the first time ever, a touchscreen, while achieving a 15% weight reduction across both 14-inch and 16-inch models. The new lineup is powered by the M5 Pro or M5 Max chip and features tandem OLED technology with 2000 nit SDR brightness, Adobe RGB support, and a new nano-texture glass option. Apple adding a touchscreen to the MacBook is a historic paradigm shift—the company has famously resisted touch on macOS for over a decade, making this one of the most significant design philosophy changes in Apple's history. The PROLED XDR display with tandem OLED technology also represents a major leap in laptop display quality, while the 15% weight reduction makes these powerful machines significantly more portable. The 14-inch model dropped from 3.5 lb (1.59 kg) to 3.0 lb (1.36 kg), while the 16-inch went from 4.7 lb (2.13 kg) to 4.0 lb (1.81 kg), with both models also becoming nearly 1mm thinner. The switch from LCD to OLED contributes to weight savings since OLED panels eliminate the backlight layer and require fewer display layers, though this alone doesn't fully account for the 300-gram reduction.

**Background**: Apple has long maintained that touchscreens don't belong on Macs, with executives including Steve Jobs and Craig Federighi repeatedly arguing that macOS is designed for precise pointer-based input rather than touch. The company instead channeled touch interaction into the iPad and iPhone ecosystems. OLED (Organic Light-Emitting Diode) displays differ from traditional LCDs by using self-emissive pixels that don't require a separate backlight, enabling thinner panels, deeper blacks, and higher contrast ratios. Apple's XDR (Extreme Dynamic Range) display branding was first introduced on the Pro Display XDR monitor and later on iPad Pro with tandem OLED, which stacks two OLED panels to achieve higher brightness levels.

## [Apple's macOS Still Trusts Compromised eMudhra CA Certificates](https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/apple.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple's macOS continues to trust compromised eMudhra certificate authority certificates at the OS level, affecting git and numerous system utilities that rely on the OS trust store. Apple's official guidance requires developers to manually edit the certificate chain, but subsequent OS updates have already reverted these mitigations at least once, reintroducing the vulnerability. This creates a significant security vulnerability for all macOS developers and users, as any system utility relying on OS-level certificate trust could be susceptible to man-in-the-middle attacks using forged certificates. The issue exposes broader problems with OS-level certificate management and Apple's inadequate mitigation strategy, especially given that Microsoft and other vendors have already taken more decisive action. The compromise involves eight eMudhra intermediate CA private keys — six associated with TLS and two with code signing. Apple's recommended patch (support.apple.com/en-us/149555) involves manually modifying the certificate trust chain via Keychain Access, but this is fragile since OS updates can revert the changes. The issue extends beyond Apple-supplied git to any utility that reads from /etc/ssl or similar OS-level certificate stores.

hackernews · galadran · Oct 28, 11:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50271640)

**Background**: eMudhra is an Indian digital certification authority that issues SSL/TLS certificates for secure web communications. When a CA's private keys are compromised, attackers can potentially forge certificates to impersonate legitimate websites or intercept encrypted traffic. Operating systems maintain trust stores at the OS level (such as /etc/ssl on macOS) that many command-line utilities rely on for certificate validation, separate from browser-specific certificate stores. Microsoft has already published an official response to the same eMudhra compromise, highlighting that this is a cross-platform industry issue.

## [Zero-Training Null Space Injection Boosts EmbeddingGemma-2 to #1 on MAEB](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1xho7pr/r_taking_embeddinggemma2_577m_from_rank_26_to_1/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A researcher projected PCA-reduced intermediate Conformer layers (Layer 2 and Layer 6) from EmbeddingGemma-2 into the 256-dimensional null space of the model's final 768×512 projection matrix, boosting the MAEB macro score from 49.3% to 54.4% and jumping from rank #26 to #1 on audio-only MAEB — all without any training. The technique exploits the fact that the text backbone's 512d hidden size leaves 256 orthogonal directions unused in the 768d output space, allowing paralinguistic information to be injected with zero interference to text embeddings. This demonstrates that heavily overtrained foundation models still contain substantial untapped signal in their intermediate layers, and that text-aligned audio encoders systematically discard paralinguistic and acoustic traits (emotion, speaker identity, timbre) during text alignment — a problem affecting the entire LLM-backed audio embedding ecosystem. The approach lets a 577M model outperform 8B-9B multimodal models on audio tasks, suggesting a lightweight, training-free path to recovering lost acoustic information that the speech embedding community can readily adopt. PCA was fitted on only 1,400 clips from 4 external audio datasets (no MAEB/MSEB data used), compressing Layer 2 and Layer 6 each to 256 dimensions before injection into the null space. Text embeddings remain bit-for-bit unchanged (text-to-audio retrieval varies by less than 0.05 points), but notable trade-offs exist: Matryoshka truncation is lost for audio (all 768 dimensions required), and spoken-intent tasks degrade because the added acoustic features slightly scale down the semantic component after normalization (e.g., SIBFLEURS spoken topic classification drops from 57.2% to 21.3%).

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/JeanMichelRanu · Oct 28, 13:34

**Background**: EmbeddingGemma-2 is Google DeepMind's open multimodal embedding model (577M parameters, Apache 2.0 license) that maps text, code, images, video, and audio into a unified 768-dimensional vector space. MAEB (Massive Audio Embedding Benchmark) is a comprehensive benchmark spanning 30 tasks across diverse audio domains, languages, and task types, including retrieval, clustering, and classification. Conformer is a convolution-augmented Transformer architecture widely used in speech processing, combining self-attention with convolution modules to capture both long-range dependencies and local patterns. When an audio encoder is aligned to a text LLM, the final layers tend to retain lexical content while filtering out paralinguistic traits, which is why intermediate layers often retain richer acoustic information.

The persistence of purchasing plans despite poor utilization indicates that strategic anxiety is overriding financial discipline in AI budget decisions.
