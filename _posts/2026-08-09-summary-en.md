## [AI-Generated Bacteriophage Genomes Validated as Viable for the First Time](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers at Arc Institute and Stanford used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to generate whole bacteriophage genomes from scratch, using the lytic phage ΦX174 as a design template. Experimental testing confirmed 16 viable phages with substantial evolutionary novelty, marking the first demonstration of AI-generated functional sequences at whole-genome scale. This is the first time AI has designed complete, functional genomes that work in the real world, bridging the gap between computational sequence generation and experimental synthetic biology. It opens the door to rapid, programmable design of phage-based therapeutics for antibiotic-resistant bacterial infections and demonstrates that genome language models can produce biologically coherent sequences at the scale of entire viral genomes. The generated phages exhibited realistic genetic architectures and desirable host tropism, meaning they were designed to infect specific bacterial hosts. The use of ΦX174—a well-characterized lytic coliphage with a small (~5.4 kb) single-stranded DNA genome—as the template provided a tractable starting point, though the approach's scalability to larger genomes remains an open question.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models (gLMs) are large language models trained on DNA sequences rather than text, treating genetic code as a biological language to learn patterns in genomic architecture. Evo 1 and Evo 2, developed by Arc Institute and Stanford, were trained on massive libraries of genetic sequences drawn from diverse organisms. Bacteriophages (phages) are viruses that specifically infect bacteria, and are increasingly explored as alternatives to antibiotics. Host tropism refers to the specificity of a pathogen for infecting particular host species or cell types, a critical property for designing phage therapeutics.
