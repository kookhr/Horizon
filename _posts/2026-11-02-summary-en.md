## [Terence Tao: Mathematics Has Changed, Let's Act Like It](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/01/mathematics-has-changed-lets-act-like-it/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Terence Tao, one of the world's most renowned mathematicians, published a blog post arguing that AI has fundamentally transformed mathematics as a discipline, and that the field's practices, credit attribution systems, and social structures must adapt to this new reality. He emphasizes that skills like choosing worthwhile problems, steering AI-assisted explorations, and digesting results will become more valuable than traditional proof construction. This represents a call for paradigm shift from arguably the most influential living mathematician, signaling that AI's impact on mathematics is not merely incremental but transformative. The discussion touches on the very social foundations of mathematics — how credit is assigned, what constitutes a meaningful contribution, and how the community should reorganize itself around capabilities that blur the line between human and machine reasoning. Tao introduces the concept of 'digestion of results' — where the mathematician's role shifts from constructing proofs to understanding AI-generated proofs thoroughly and using them to develop further questions and directions. He argues that someone who chooses a worthwhile problem, obtains a proof via AI, understands it completely, and derives new directions deserves substantial credit even without contributing a new ingredient to the original proof.
